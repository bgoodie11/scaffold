About this build

A routing experiment for sidewalk-shed city life.

ScaffoldMax compares nearby walking routes, then scores each one by its likely exposure to active NYC sidewalk sheds.

Data
NYC DOB active sidewalk-shed permits, OpenStreetMap routes and NYC building footprints.
Approach
Test nearby parallel streets, infer likely building frontage and keep detours within 12% of the shortest route.
Limits
Permit locations and inferred cover are approximate. This is a prototype, not a guarantee that a block is passable or dry.
